Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

At Cozy Plate Recipes (accessible from https://hockeyworldcup2026schedule.com), your privacy is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by Cozy Plate Recipes and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit Cozy Plate Recipes, we may collect information in two ways:

  • Log Files: Like most standard websites, we utilize log files. The information inside log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This data is used solely to analyze trends, administer the site, track user movement around the site, and gather broad demographic information.
  • Voluntary Information: If you contact us via our contact form or direct email, we collect your name and email address to respond directly to your inquiry. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal email address.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Cozy Plate Recipes uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences, to record user-specific information on which pages the user accesses or visits, and to customize web page content based on visitors' browser type or other information that the visitor sends via their browser.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to this website and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in the advertisements and links that appear on Cozy Plate Recipes. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.
  • Other technologies (such as cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons) may also be used by the third-party ad networks to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.
  • Visitors may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.

Please note: Cozy Plate Recipes has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand website traffic, bounce rates, popular recipe pages, and visitor engagement. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data using cookies. You can prevent Google Analytics from recognizing you on return visits by disabling cookies on your browser or using the official Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Pinterest and Social Media Plugins

Our website features Pinterest "Save" buttons and social sharing tools to allow users to pin recipes to their Pinterest boards. These social platforms may track your interaction with the embedded buttons according to their respective privacy policies.

6. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose what personal data it has collected, delete personal data collected, and not sell the consumer's personal data. If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

7. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following: the right to access, the right to rectification, the right to erasure, the right to restrict processing, the right to object to processing, and the right to data portability. Please contact us if you wish to exercise any of these rights.

8. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. Cozy Plate Recipes does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.
