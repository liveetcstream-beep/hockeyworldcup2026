1. Classic Peanut Butter Blossoms on a Baking Mat

1. Classic Peanut Butter Blossoms on a Baking Mat

⏱️ 25 Mins (10m prep, 15m bake) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$2.20/serving) 🍽️ Serves 12‑15
Save Pin
Warm, chewy cookies spread across a non‑stick mat, each crowned with a shiny Hershey’s Kiss.

These classic blossoms marry the nutty richness of creamy peanut butter with the buttery melt of milk chocolate, creating a sweet‑savory harmony that’s instantly comforting. The cookie’s soft center stays tender while the edges turn lightly crisp, releasing a toasted aroma that fills the kitchen. A subtle hint of vanilla rounds out the flavor, and the chocolate kiss adds a glossy finish that catches the holiday lights. Guests love the contrast of textures—soft bite, crisp edge, and smooth chocolate melt.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all‑purpose flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ cup chopped peanuts (optional)
  • Hershey’s milk chocolate kisses (one per cookie)

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a silicone baking mat on a full‑size sheet pan
  2. Step 2: in a large bowl, beat the softened butter, peanut butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar together on medium speed until light and fluffy, about 2‑3 minutes
  3. Step 3: add the egg and vanilla, mixing until fully incorporated, then whisk in the flour, baking soda, and salt until a smooth dough forms, stopping just before the dough looks glossy
  4. Step 4: fold in optional chopped peanuts for extra crunch
  5. Step 5: scoop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the prepared mat, spacing 2 inches apart, and bake for 8‑10 minutes until the edges turn a light golden brown and the centers still look slightly soft
  6. Step 6: remove from oven, immediately press a chocolate kiss into the center of each cookie while still warm, allowing it to melt slightly
  7. Step 7: transfer the sheet to a cooling rack and let the cookies set for 5 minutes before moving them completely to the rack to finish cooling.
💡 Chef's Tip: Chill the dough for 15 minutes before scooping; this firms it up, prevents spreading, and yields perfectly round blossoms.

Serve warm with a glass of cold milk or a hot mug of cocoa; leftover cookies store in an airtight tin for up to 5 days.

2. Overhead‑Cool Peanut Butter Blossoms on a Baking Sheet

Save Pin
A top‑down shot of perfectly spaced cookies cooling, each topped with a shiny chocolate kiss.

This version emphasizes a slightly crispier edge while keeping the interior ultra‑soft, thanks to a brief extra bake and a cool‑down on the sheet. The buttery peanut base is amplified by a whisper of sea salt, creating a sweet‑salty pop that pairs beautifully with the creamy chocolate kiss. The aroma of toasted peanuts and caramelized sugar fills the kitchen, making it impossible to resist reaching for another. Ideal for holiday platters where each cookie can be lifted with a tongs for a mess‑free serving.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups all‑purpose flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ tsp fine sea salt
  • Hershey’s kisses
  • parchment paper

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a full‑size baking sheet with parchment
  2. Step 2: in a mixing bowl, cream together the butter, peanut butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar on medium speed until pale and fluffy, about 3 minutes
  3. Step 3: beat in the egg and vanilla until just combined
  4. Step 4: whisk together flour, baking soda, and sea salt, then gradually add to the wet mixture, mixing just until a cohesive dough forms
  5. Step 5: using a cookie scoop, drop rounded mounds onto the parchment, spacing 2 inches apart
  6. Step 6: bake for 12‑14 minutes until the edges turn a light amber while the centers still appear slightly under‑baked
  7. Step 7: immediately after removal, press a chocolate kiss into each warm cookie
  8. Step 8: allow the sheet to sit for 3 minutes so the kisses set, then transfer cookies to a cooling rack to finish cooling.
💡 Chef's Tip: Sprinkle a pinch of flaky sea salt on each kiss right after pressing for an elegant sweet‑salty finish.

Arrange on a festive platter with fresh cranberries for color contrast; store in a zip‑top bag with a paper towel to absorb excess moisture.

9. Batch‑Size Peanut Butter Blossoms on Parchment

⏱️ 30 Mins (12m prep, 18m bake) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$2.20/serving) 🍽️ Serves 24‑30 cookies
Save Pin
A generous batch of classic peanut butter blossoms, baked to a perfect buttery crunch.

These cookies marry the rich, nutty depth of peanut butter with a sweet, melt‑in‑your‑mouth chocolate kiss. The dough stays soft and chewy in the center while the edges turn lightly crisp, releasing a warm, toasted aroma. A hint of vanilla and a pinch of sea salt elevate the flavor, making each bite a comforting holiday treat that disappears fast.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all‑purpose flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ tsp baking powder
  • pinch of salt
  • chocolate kiss candies (about 30)

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment paper

A cozy, rustic arrangement of stacked cookies with loose chocolates nearby, evoking a warm, homey holiday atmosphere.

This recipe focuses on the authentic, unadulterated taste of high-quality peanut butter and dark chocolate. The flavor is deep and rich, with the nuttiness of the peanut butter balancing the intense cocoa notes of the loose kisses. The texture is airy on the outside with a dense, cake-like interior that holds the chocolate perfectly. This presentation is a hit because the loose kisses allow guests to customize their bite, choosing how much chocolate they want in each cookie.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1.5 cups smooth peanut butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 egg
  • 30 Hershey's Kisses (unwrapped)
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat the oven to 325°F (165°C), which is a slightly lower temperature to promote a softer, chewier consistency rather than a crisp one. Cream the butter, brown sugar, and peanut butter in a large bowl until the mixture is thick and creamy, ensuring no lumps remain. Add the egg, vanilla, and cinnamon, mixing well, then gradually stir in the flour until the dough holds together when squeezed. Roll the dough into 1-inch balls and place them 2 inches apart on ungreased baking sheets for maximum browning. Bake for 12-14 minutes until the cookies have spread and the centers are set but still look slightly underdone, as they will continue to cook on the hot sheet. Remove from the oven and immediately press an unwrapped kiss into the center of each warm cookie, using the back of a spoon to gently flatten the chocolate and create a seal. Allow the cookies to cool on the baking sheet for 10 minutes to harden, which is crucial for preventing the chocolate from sliding out. Once fully cooled, stack them in clear jars or on a white plate for a rustic, inviting display.
💡 Chef's Tip: Using brown sugar instead of white adds moisture and a caramel-like depth that pairs exceptionally well with the cinnamon.

These cookies freeze well; place them on a tray to freeze solid, then store in a freezer bag for up to 3 months. Thaw at room temperature for 30 minutes before serving.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What foods should I prepare ahead of time?
A: Cold dips, truffle balls, snack mixes, and chocolate treats can easily be prepared 1-2 days in advance. Hot appetizers like wrapped sausages can be assembled ahead and baked fresh right as guests arrive.

Q: How much food should I plan per adult guest?
A: For a 2-3 hour cocktail party where a sit-down dinner is not served, plan on 6 to 8 appetizer bites per adult. If hosting during a traditional dinner hour, increase to 10 to 12 pieces per person.

Q: What foods can safely stay at room temperature?
A: Dry snacks and baked treats can stay out on the buffet table throughout the party. For perishable appetizers (cheeses, meats, and dips), adhere to the standard USDA 2-hour rule by serving in smaller batches replenished from the fridge.
