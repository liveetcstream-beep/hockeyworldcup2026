1. Ree Drummond’s Ultimate Cheesy Bacon Hashbrown Casserole

1. Ree Drummond’s Ultimate Cheesy Bacon Hashbrown Casserole

⏱️ 45 Mins (10m prep, 35m cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$3.00/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8-10
Save Pin
A generous layer of crispy bacon and bubbling cheddar cheese crowns this comforting holiday classic.

This dish delivers a robust, savory punch with the smoky depth of cooked bacon and the rich creaminess of sour cream. The frozen hashbrowns transform into a fluffy, tender base that absorbs the savory egg mixture perfectly. The top crust achieves a satisfying golden crispness that shatters beautifully against the soft interior. It is an absolute hit with guests because it combines the best of a diner breakfast with the indulgence of a holiday feast.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 32 oz frozen shredded hashbrowns
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 lb bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup sliced green onions

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat your oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit and generously grease a 9x13 inch baking dish with cooking spray to prevent sticking
  2. Step 2: in a large mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, and sour cream until fully combined and smooth
  3. Step 3: stir in the crumbled bacon, half of the shredded cheddar cheese, and the green onions into the liquid mixture, ensuring the bacon is evenly distributed
  4. Step 4: pour the frozen shredded hashbrowns directly into the prepared baking dish and spread them out into an even layer without thawing
  5. Step 5: pour the egg and bacon mixture over the hashbrowns, using a spatula to gently press the hashbrowns down into the liquid so they are completely saturated
  6. Step 6: sprinkle the remaining cup of cheddar cheese evenly over the top surface of the casserole
  7. Step 7: bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until the center is set, the edges are bubbling, and the top cheese is melted and golden brown
  8. Step 8: let the casserole cool for at least 10 minutes before slicing to ensure clean, stable portions.
💡 Chef's Tip: For the best texture, use frozen shredded hashbrowns straight from the freezer; this prevents the dish from becoming soupy and ensures a fluffy, distinct hashbrown structure rather than a pureed potato texture.

Serve immediately with a side of fresh fruit salad to cut through the richness and fat. This casserole freezes beautifully for up to two months; simply wrap tightly in plastic wrap and foil before freezing and bake straight from frozen for an additional 15 minutes.

2. Cracker Barrel Style Baked Hashbrowns with Plated Eggs

⏱️ 50 Mins (15m prep, 35m cook) 💰 Mid-Range (~$4.50/serving) 🍽️ Serves 6
Save Pin
A rustic presentation of baked hashbrowns topped with perfectly fried eggs and crisp bacon.

This recipe captures the iconic diner experience with a comforting, buttery potato base that is crisp on the outside and tender within. The runny yolk from the plated egg acts as a rich, golden sauce that adds luxurious moisture to every bite. Crispy bacon strips provide a salty, smoky contrast that elevates the simple potato flavor. The aroma of baking potatoes and frying bacon fills the kitchen, signaling a hearty and satisfying holiday morning.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 24 oz frozen shelled hashbrowns
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 6 large eggs
  • 6 slices bacon, cooked until crisp

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat your oven to 400 degrees Fahrenheit and spray a 9x13 inch baking dish with non-stick spray
  2. Step 2: combine the frozen shelled hashbrowns, melted butter, chicken broth, garlic powder, and onion powder in a large bowl, tossing gently to coat each piece evenly
  3. Step 3: spread the seasoned hashbrowns into a single, even layer in the prepared baking dish, pressing down slightly to create a compact base
  4. Step 4: bake for 25 minutes, or until the hashbrowns are golden brown and the liquid has been absorbed
  5. Step 5: while the hashbrowns bake, fry your eggs in a non-stick skillet over medium heat until the whites are set but the yolks remain runny
  6. Step 6: cook the bacon in the same skillet until deeply crisp and drained on paper towels
  7. Step 7: remove the hashbrowns from the oven and immediately top each serving portion with a fried egg and a strip of bacon before serving.
💡 Chef's Tip: Use shelled hashbrowns for this specific recipe as they provide a chunkier, more substantial texture that holds up better when topped with heavy eggs and bacon compared to shredded versions.

This dish is best served immediately to maintain the runny yolk and crisp bacon texture. For a make-ahead option, prepare the hashbrown base and keep it refrigerated, then reheat in a 350-degree oven for 15 minutes before topping with fresh eggs and bacon.

3. Copycat Golden Top Hashbrown Casserole

⏱️ 40 Mins (10m prep, 30m cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$2.75/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8
Save Pin
A perfectly even, golden-brown crust crowns this creamy and savory holiday breakfast staple.

The defining feature of this dish is its stunningly uniform golden crust that offers a delicate crunch before yielding to a creamy, savory interior. The flavor profile is balanced and comforting, relying on the natural sweetness of the potatoes and the richness of the dairy. There are no overpowering spices, allowing the pure, buttery potato flavor to shine through. It is a crowd-pleaser because it feels festive and homemade without being overly complex or heavy.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 32 oz frozen shredded hashbrowns
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup milk
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup shredded mild cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup sliced green onions
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat your oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit and grease a 9x13 inch baking dish with butter or non-stick spray
  2. Step 2: in a medium bowl, whisk together the milk, sour cream, eggs, salt, and black pepper until the mixture is smooth and uniform
  3. Step 3: pour the frozen shredded hashbrowns into the baking dish and spread them evenly, ensuring no large clumps remain
  4. Step 4: pour the liquid egg mixture over the hashbrowns and stir gently with a fork to distribute the liquid throughout the potato mixture
  5. Step 5: sprinkle the shredded cheddar cheese and green onions evenly over the top surface, creating a consistent layer
  6. Step 6: bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until the top is a deep golden brown and the center is set when shaken
  7. Step 7: allow the casserole to rest for 5 minutes before cutting to allow the structure to firm up for clean slices.
💡 Chef's Tip: For an even more golden top, switch your oven to broil for the last 2 minutes of cooking, watching closely to ensure the cheese browns without burning.

Pair this casserole with fresh berries and yogurt for a lighter, balanced plate. This recipe can be prepared the night before, covered, and refrigerated; simply add an extra 10 minutes to the baking time when cooking from a cold start.

4. Kardea Style Sour Cream & Chive Hashbrown Casserole

⏱️ 45 Mins (15m prep, 30m cook) 💰 Mid-Range (~$3.50/serving) 🍽️ Serves 6
Save Pin
A tender slice of casserole topped with fresh chives and a cool dollop of sour cream.

This recipe leans into fresh, herbaceous notes with the addition of chopped chives that provide a mild oniony bite. The sour cream base creates a velvety, tangy counterpoint to the rich, starchy potatoes. The texture is incredibly tender, almost custard-like, making it a sophisticated take on a classic breakfast item. It appeals to guests who prefer a lighter, fresher flavor profile amidst the heavier holiday fare.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 32 oz frozen shredded hashbrowns
  • 1.5 cups sour cream
  • 1/2 cup buttermilk
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup fresh chives, chopped
  • 1/2 cup shredded white cheddar cheese
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • pinch of salt and pepper

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat your oven to 375 degrees Fahrenheit and prepare a 9x13 inch baking dish with non-stick spray
  2. Step 2: in a large bowl, combine the sour cream, buttermilk, eggs, and garlic powder, whisking until completely smooth
  3. Step 3: stir in the chopped chives and white cheddar cheese into the dairy mixture
  4. Step 4: add the frozen shredded hashbrowns to the bowl and mix gently until the potatoes are thoroughly coated in the creamy mixture
  5. Step 5: transfer the mixture to the prepared baking dish and smooth the top with the back of a spoon
  6. Step 6: bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until the edges are set and the top is lightly golden
  7. Step 7: let cool for 10 minutes, then cut into squares and serve with an additional dollop of sour cream and a sprinkle of fresh chives.
💡 Chef's Tip: Using buttermilk instead of regular milk adds a subtle tang that enhances the sour cream flavor and helps tenderize the potatoes further.

Serve with a side of fresh sliced peaches or strawberries to complement the creamy texture. This casserole holds up well in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat individual portions in the microwave for 1-2 minutes until warm throughout.

5. Katie Lee Slow Cooker Ham & Peppers Hashbrown Casserole

⏱️ 8 Hours (15m prep, 8h cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$2.25/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8-10
Save Pin
A hearty slow cooker dish featuring savory ham, colorful peppers, and fluffy hashbrowns.

The slow cooking process allows the flavors of the ham and peppers to meld deeply into the potato base, creating a savory, umami-rich dish. The sweetness of the bell peppers balances the saltiness of the ham, resulting in a complex flavor profile. The hashbrowns become incredibly tender and absorb the savory juices from the meat and vegetables. This is a low-effort, high-reward option that ensures a hot, ready-to-serve breakfast with minimal morning stress.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 32 oz frozen shredded hashbrowns
  • 1 cup diced cooked ham
  • 1 cup diced bell peppers (red and green)
  • 1 cup diced onion
  • 1 can cream of chicken soup (10.5 oz)
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In the slow cooker, combine the frozen shredded hashbrowns, diced ham, bell peppers, onions, cream of chicken soup, and milk
  2. Step 2: stir gently to distribute the ingredients evenly, ensuring the liquid covers the bottom of the pot
  3. Step 3: cover and cook on low for 8 hours or on high for 4 hours, stirring once halfway through to prevent sticking
  4. Step 4: after the cooking time, check the consistency
  5. Step 5: if it is too thick, stir in a few extra tablespoons of milk to reach the desired creamy texture
  6. Step 6: sprinkle the shredded cheddar cheese over the top and cover for an additional 5 minutes to melt the cheese
  7. Step 7: serve directly from the slow cooker or transfer to a serving platter, garnishing with fresh parsley if desired.
💡 Chef's Tip: To make this an overnight prep, assemble all ingredients in the slow cooker the night before, cover, and refrigerate; in the morning, simply plug it in and let it cook while you get ready.

This dish is excellent for large gatherings as it can be kept warm in the slow cooker for several hours. It stores well in the refrigerator for up to 4 days and can be reheated in small batches in the microwave.

2. Individual Hash Brown Breakfast Bundt Cakes

6. Individual Hash Brown Breakfast Bundt Cakes

⏱️ 45 Mins (15m prep, 30m cook) 💰 Mid-Range (~$3.25/serving) 🍽️ Serves 6-8
Save Pin
Elegant individual bundt cakes made from hashbrowns, topped with savory bacon and herbs.

This unique presentation turns a standard casserole into an elegant, individual serving that looks impressive at a brunch table. The ring shape ensures even cooking, resulting in a crispy exterior and a moist, savory center. The flavor is classic and comforting, with the addition of bacon bits providing a smoky crunch. It is a hit with guests because it offers a fun, visually appealing twist on a familiar favorite, making it easy to eat and share.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 32 oz frozen shredded hashbrowns
  • 4 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup sour cream
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup cooked bacon bits
  • 1/4 cup sliced green onions

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat your oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit and generously grease a 6-cup mini bundt pan with cooking spray
  2. Step 2: in a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, sour cream, and milk until smooth
  3. Step 3: add the frozen shredded hashbrowns, shredded cheddar, bacon bits, and green onions to the bowl, mixing until all ingredients are evenly distributed
  4. Step 4: divide the mixture evenly among the prepared mini bundt cups, filling each about 3/4 full
  5. Step 5: smooth the tops with the back of a fork and sprinkle with any remaining cheese or bacon bits for garnish
  6. Step 6: bake for 25 to 30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean and the edges are golden brown
  7. Step 7: let cool in the pans for 5 minutes, then invert onto a serving platter to release the bundts.
💡 Chef's Tip: Using mini bundt pans instead of a large sheet pan allows for individual portions and ensures a higher surface area to volume ratio, resulting in a crispier texture around the edges.

These bundts are perfect for appetizer beginnings or as a main course; serve with a side of fresh fruit and berries. They can be frozen uncooked in the bundt pans; wrap in foil and freeze, then bake straight from frozen for an additional 10-15 minutes.

7. Bacon‑Infused Hashbrown Egg Bake

⏱️ 45 Mins (15m prep, 30m bake) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$3.00/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8
Save Pin
A golden‑brown hashbrown casserole crowned with runny eggs and crispy bacon, perfect for a festive breakfast.

This casserole brings together the comforting heft of hashbrowns with the savory crunch of bacon and the silky richness of eggs. The golden top offers a satisfying crunch while the interior remains moist and custard‑like. The aroma of sizzling bacon mingles with the gentle scent of melted cheddar, making it a crowd‑pleaser at any holiday table. It’s a simple yet indulgent dish that feels like a warm hug in every bite.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1½ lb frozen hashbrown patties
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • ½ cup milk
  • 6 slices thick‑cut bacon
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
  2. Step 2: grease a 9×13 inch baking dish
  3. Step 3: arrange frozen hashbrown patties in a single layer
  4. Step 4: In a large bowl, whisk eggs, milk, salt, pepper until well blended
  5. Step 5: pour over hashbrowns, ensuring even coverage
  6. Step 6: Sprinkle shredded cheddar evenly across the top, then scatter bacon slices over the cheese
  7. Step 7: Cover dish with foil and bake for 25 minutes
  8. Step 8: remove foil, bake another 15 minutes until eggs set, cheese melted, and top golden brown
  9. Step 9: Let stand 5 minutes, then cut into squares, serve warm with a drizzle of hollandaise or a side of fresh herbs.
💡 Chef's Tip: For a crispier bacon top, pre‑cook bacon until just browned, then lay it over the casserole before baking.

Serve with a side of fresh fruit or a light green salad to balance richness. Store leftovers in a sealed container for up to 3 days and reheat in the oven at 350°F until warmed through.

8. Amish Breakfast Casserole Slice

⏱️ 1 hr (20m prep, 40m bake) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$2.75/serving) 🍽️ Serves 10
Save Pin
A comforting slice of Amish breakfast casserole featuring layers of hashbrowns, eggs, and sausage, topped with a golden crust.

This classic Amish casserole blends the heartiness of hashbrowns with the savory depth of breakfast sausage, all wrapped in a creamy egg custard. The golden crust offers a satisfying crunch, while the interior stays moist and flavorful. The gentle aroma of sautéed onions and peppered eggs invites everyone to gather around the table. It’s a timeless dish that brings a sense of home and tradition to any holiday gathering.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 lb breakfast sausage
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 cups frozen hashbrown patties
  • 1 cup diced onions
  • 1 tsp salt

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
  2. Step 2: grease a 9×13 inch baking dish
  3. Step 3: In a skillet, cook breakfast sausage over medium heat until browned, crumble, and set aside
  4. Step 4: In same skillet, sauté diced onions until translucent, then stir in diced bell peppers for 2 minutes
  5. Step 5: In a bowl, whisk eggs, milk, salt, pepper
  6. Step 6: fold in shredded cheddar
  7. Step 7: Layer hashbrown patties in dish, sprinkle sausage and sautéed veggies, pour egg mixture over
  8. Step 8: Cover with foil and bake 30 minutes
  9. Step 9: remove foil, bake 10 minutes until top is golden and bubbly
  10. Step 10: Let cool 5 minutes before slicing.
💡 Chef's Tip: Add a handful of chopped fresh herbs like parsley or chives before baking for a bright, fresh finish.

Serve warm with a dollop of sour cream or a side of fresh tomato salsa. Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 4 days and can be reheated in a toaster oven.

9. Hashbrown Quiche Pie

⏱️ 1 hr 10 min (25m prep, 45m bake) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$3.50/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8
Save Pin
A savory hashbrown quiche pie with a buttery crust and a silky custard, perfect for brunch.

This quiche marries the buttery crunch of a flaky crust with a velvety custard that carries the earthy flavor of hashbrowns. The shredded cheddar melts into a creamy base, while the golden top glistens like a sunrise. The dish offers a delightful contrast of textures—from the crisp pastry to the smooth interior—and a comforting, homestyle taste that warms the soul. It’s an elegant yet approachable centerpiece for any holiday meal.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 9‑inch pie crust
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 2 cups frozen hashbrown patties
  • 1 tsp salt

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
  2. Step 2: roll out pie crust and press into a 9‑inch pie dish, trim edges
  3. Step 3: Arrange frozen hashbrown patties in a single layer on the crust, then sprinkle shredded cheddar evenly
  4. Step 4: In a medium bowl, whisk eggs, milk, heavy cream, salt, pepper until smooth
  5. Step 5: Pour custard mixture over hashbrowns, ensuring even distribution
  6. Step 6: Cover with foil to prevent browning
  7. Step 7: Bake 35 minutes, remove foil, bake 10 more minutes until custard is set and top is golden brown
  8. Step 8: Let cool 10 minutes before slicing.
💡 Chef's Tip: For extra crispness, pre‑toast the hashbrowns in a skillet for 2 minutes before layering.

Serve warm with a side of mixed greens dressed in a light vinaigrette. Store leftovers in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheat in a microwave until warmed through.

A comforting casserole featuring golden breadcrumb topping, melty cheese, and tender hashbrowns, reminiscent of homestyle comfort.

The buttery crumbs add a nutty flavor that pairs beautifully with the mild cheese, creating a harmonious blend of textures.   • 2 cups frozen hashbrown patties

  8. Step 8: let rest 5 minutes before serving.

13. Delish Egg, Sausage & Spinach Hashbrown Casserole

⏱️ 50 Mins (15m prep, 35m cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$3.20/serving) 🍽️ Serves 6-8
Save Pin
A hearty, green-tinged casserole where savory sausage and earthy spinach meld with crispy hashbrown edges.

This dish balances the rich, fatty punch of browned breakfast sausage with the fresh, vegetal bite of wilted spinach. The eggs bind the layers into a cohesive, fluffy interior while the hashbrowns provide a satisfying crunch on top. It is a nutrient-dense morning favorite that feels indulgent yet wholesome. Guests love it because it offers a vibrant color contrast and a complex flavor profile that stands out from standard cheesy casseroles.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 16 oz frozen shredded hashbrowns, thawed
  • 12 large eggs, beaten
  • 8 oz fresh spinach, chopped
  • 8 oz breakfast sausage, cooked and crumbled
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • salt, pepper, and garlic powder

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C) and lightly grease a 9x13 inch baking dish with non-stick spray or butter. In a large bowl, combine the thawed hashbrowns, beaten eggs, heavy cream, and seasonings, mixing well to ensure every potato shard is coated in the custard. Spread half of this mixture evenly into the prepared baking dish, then scatter half of the cooked sausage and chopped spinach over the top. Pour the remaining hashbrown-egg mixture over the vegetables and meat, distributing it as evenly as possible to create distinct layers. Bake for 30-35 minutes until the center is set and the top is bubbling and golden brown. Remove from the oven, sprinkle generously with shredded mozzarella, and return to the oven for an additional 5 minutes until the cheese is melted and slightly browned.
💡 Chef's Tip: Squeeze excess water from the fresh spinach before adding it to the casserole to prevent the bottom layer from becoming soggy during the baking process.

Serve immediately with a side of fresh fruit salad to cut through the richness. This dish can be assembled the night before and refrigerated tightly covered, then baked in the morning.

14. Crispy Breadcrumb Topped Hashbrown Casserole

A spoonful reveals the creamy interior beneath a shatteringly crisp, golden breadcrumb crust.

This recipe elevates the classic casserole by adding a sophisticated, crunchy textural element via a seasoned breadcrumb topping. The base is a rich, creamy potato and cheese blend that offers comfort, while the top provides a savory, toasted crunch. Top with the remaining hashbrowns, bacon, and cheese, ensuring an even distribution of toppings. Bake for 40-45 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the center is set.
💡 Chef's Tip: Cook the bacon beforehand and drain it thoroughly to avoid making the casserole greasy; save the bacon fat to use in the egg mixture for extra flavor.

Serve with a side of fresh fruit or a light green salad to complement the rich flavors. This casserole can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated in the oven until warm.

29. Sausage Gravy Loaded Hashbrown Casserole

⏱️ 50 Mins (15m prep, 35m cook) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$3.50/serving) 🍽️ Serves 6-8
Save Pin
A comforting bake topped with a generous layer of creamy sausage gravy, creating a hearty and indulgent breakfast dish.

This recipe combines the classic comfort of banded hashbrowns with the rich, savory taste of homemade sausage gravy. The flavor profile is deeply savory, with notes of garlic, onion, and seasoned pork sausage. The texture is a delightful mix of the crispy hashbrown top and the smooth, creamy gravy that soaks into the layers. It is a hit with guests because it offers a familiar, nostalgic taste that is both filling and satisfying.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 lbs frozen shredded hashbrowns, thawed
  • 1 lb pork sausage, cooked and crumbled
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp garlic powder

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13-inch baking dish. Press the thawed hashbrowns into the bottom of the dish, creating an even layer. To make the gravy, melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat and whisk in the flour, cooking for 2 minutes to remove the raw taste. Slowly whisk in the milk, chicken broth, and heavy cream, stirring constantly until the mixture thickens into a smooth gravy. Add the crumbled sausage and garlic powder to the gravy, stirring to combine, and let it simmer for 5 minutes. Pour the sausage gravy evenly over the hashbrowns in the baking dish. Bake for 35-40 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the gravy is bubbling around the edges.
💡 Chef's Tip: For a thicker gravy, use a roux with equal parts flour and butter; for a lighter version, reduce the flour and add more broth.

Serve with a side of fresh fruit or a light salad to balance the richness of the gravy. This casserole can be made ahead and refrigerated, then reheated in the oven until hot and bubbly.

30. Corned Beef & Cabbage Crispy Top Casserole

⏱️ 60 Mins (20m prep, 40m cook) 💰 Mid-Range (~$5.00/serving) 🍽️ Serves 6-8
Save Pin
A festive casserole topped with crispy corned beef, shredded cabbage, and a golden cheese crust, perfect for a holiday breakfast.

This unique twist on the traditional hashbrown casserole incorporates the salty, savory flavor of corned beef and the mild sweetness of cabbage. The taste is robust and hearty, with a hint of spice from the corned beef seasoning. The texture is a combination of the crispy hashbrown top, the tender cabbage, and the juicy corned beef. It is a hit with guests because it offers a novel and flavorful take on a classic dish, making it a memorable part of the holiday morning.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 lbs frozen shredded hashbrowns, thawed
  • 1 lb corned beef, sliced and cooked
  • 1 head green cabbage, shredded
  • 6 large eggs
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 2 cups shredded Swiss cheese
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13-inch baking dish. Press the thawed hashbrowns into the bottom of the dish, creating an even layer. Spread the shredded cabbage evenly over the hashbrowns, followed by the sliced corned beef. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, and caraway seeds until smooth, then pour this mixture over the corned beef. Sprinkle the shredded Swiss cheese evenly over the top, covering the surface completely. Bake for 40-45 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the center is set.
💡 Chef's Tip: Use pre-cooked corned beef to save time; if using raw, ensure it is fully cooked before adding to the casserole.

Serve with a side of fresh fruit or a light salad to complement the savory flavors. This casserole can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated in the oven until warm.

Essential Party Food Prep & Hosting Secrets

  • Prep Cold Platters in Advance: Assemble charcuterie boards, dips, and desserts earlier in the day, cover with cling wrap, and chill so you can relax with guests.
  • Stagger Hot Oven Appetizers: Keep baking sheets with pre-assembled finger foods in the fridge and bake in waves so fresh, warm snacks flow continuously.
  • Smart Grocery Budget Swaps: Purchasing block cheese to shred or cube at home delivers superior melting and noticeably trims your grocery bill compared to pre-shredded bags.
  • Strategic Grazing Stations: Separate savory finger foods, dips, and desserts onto different tables to prevent kitchen bottlenecks.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What foods should I prepare ahead of time?
A: Cold dips, truffle balls, snack mixes, and chocolate treats can easily be prepared 1-2 days in advance. Hot appetizers like wrapped sausages can be assembled ahead and baked fresh right as guests arrive.

Q: How much food should I plan per adult guest?
A: For a 2-3 hour cocktail party where a sit-down dinner is not served, plan on 6 to 8 appetizer bites per adult. If hosting during a traditional dinner hour, increase to 10 to 12 pieces per person.

Q: What foods can safely stay at room temperature?
A: Dry snacks and baked treats can stay out on the buffet table throughout the party. For perishable appetizers (cheeses, meats, and dips), adhere to the standard USDA 2-hour rule by serving in smaller batches replenished from the fridge.
