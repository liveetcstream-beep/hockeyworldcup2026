1. Snowmen, Trees & Stockings Sugar Cookie Medley

1. Snowmen, Trees & Stockings Sugar Cookie Medley

⏱️ 30 Mins (15m prep, 15m bake) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$3.00/serving) 🍽️ Serves 12-15
Save Pin
A joyful trio of holiday shapes dusted with sparkling royal icing and colorful sprinkles.

These classic sugar cookies boast a buttery base that melts on the tongue, accented by a light vanilla perfume. The royal icing adds a sweet, slightly tangy contrast, while the sprinkles provide a satisfying crunch. Each shape—snowman, tree, or stocking—offers a different visual delight, making the batch a crowd‑pleasing centerpiece at any holiday gathering.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 ½ cups all‑purpose flour
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • pinch of salt
  • royal icing (powdered sugar, meringue powder, milk)
  • assorted sprinkles

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a large mixing bowl, cream the softened butter and granulated sugar together on medium speed until pale and fluffy, about 2‑3 minutes
  2. Step 2: add the egg and vanilla extract, beating until fully incorporated
  3. Step 3: whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt in a separate bowl, then gradually add to the butter mixture, mixing on low until a smooth dough forms
  4. Step 4: divide the dough into two portions, wrap in plastic, and chill for at least 30 minutes to firm
  5. Step 5: preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line baking sheets with parchment
  6. Step 6: roll out the chilled dough to ¼‑inch thickness on a lightly floured surface and cut into snowmen, tree and stocking shapes using festive cookie cutters
  7. Step 7: place cookies on the prepared sheets, leaving 1 inch of space, and bake for 9‑11 minutes until the edges are just golden and the centers are set
  8. Step 8: allow cookies to cool completely on a wire rack before decorating with royal icing and sprinkles.
💡 Chef's Tip: Chill the dough thoroughly—at least 30 minutes—to prevent spreading and to achieve crisp edges with a soft center.

Arrange the decorated cookies on a holiday platter with fresh pine sprigs for extra charm; store any leftovers in an airtight tin at room temperature for up to 5 days.

2. Frosted Blue Snowflake Sugar Cookies

⏱️ 28 Mins (12m prep, 16m bake) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$2.80/serving) 🍽️ Serves 10-12
Save Pin
Delicate snowflake cookies glazed in icy blue royal icing and dusted with edible glitter.

These snowflake cookies are a wintry wonder, featuring a buttery, melt‑in‑your‑mouth base and a cool, slightly tangy blue royal icing that mimics fresh snowfall. The subtle vanilla aroma pairs perfectly with the faint citrus zest hidden in the icing, while the edible glitter adds a magical sparkle. Their crisp edges and soft centers make them irresistible for both kids and adults.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all‑purpose flour
  • ¾ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ tsp almond extract
  • blue food‑grade gel coloring
  • royal icing (powdered sugar, meringue powder, milk, blue gel)
  • edible glitter

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Combine butter and powdered sugar in a stand mixer on medium speed, beating until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes
  2. Step 2: blend in the egg yolk, vanilla, and almond extracts until smooth
  3. Step 3: sift flour and a pinch of salt into the bowl, mixing on low until a cohesive dough forms
  4. Step 4: shape the dough into a disc, wrap, and chill for 20 minutes
  5. Step 5: preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two baking sheets with silicone mats
  6. Step 6: roll dough to ¼‑inch thickness, cut snowflake shapes with a delicate cutter, and place on the mats
  7. Step 7: bake for 10‑12 minutes until the edges turn a pale gold and the cookies are firm to the touch
  8. Step 8: while cooling, whisk together royal icing ingredients, adding blue gel until the desired shade is reached, then pipe intricate snowflake patterns and sprinkle with edible glitter before the icing sets.
💡 Chef's Tip: Add a drop of lemon juice to the icing for a subtle bright note that balances the sweetness.

Serve the snowflakes on a mirrored tray for extra sparkle; they keep well in a sealed container at room temperature for up to a week.

3. Blue Snowflake Cookie Mug Treat

⏱️ 25 Mins (10m prep, 15m bake) 💰 Budget-Friendly (~$2.50/serving) 🍽️ Serves 8-10
Save Pin
A single blue‑iced snowflake cookie paired with a cozy mug of hot cocoa.

This single‑serve snowflake cookie delivers the same buttery, vanilla‑kissed base as the platter version, but its compact size makes it perfect for dunking into hot cocoa. The blue royal icing adds a refreshing chill that contrasts beautifully with the warmth of the drink, while a light dusting of powdered sugar mimics fresh snow. The crisp‑on‑the‑outside, soft‑inside texture creates a satisfying bite every time.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 1 ¼ cups all‑purpose flour
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 egg white
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • blue gel food coloring
  • royal icing (powdered sugar, meringue powder, milk, blue gel)
  • pinch of salt

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Whisk together flour and salt in a small bowl
  2. Step 2: in a separate bowl, beat butter and sugar on medium speed until creamy, then add egg white and vanilla, mixing until combined
  3. Step 3: gradually incorporate the dry flour mixture, stirring until a smooth dough forms
  4. Step 4: add a few drops of blue gel and knead gently until the color is evenly distributed
  5. Step 5: roll the dough into a ¼‑inch thick slab and cut out snowflake shapes using a small cutter
  6. Step 6: place cookies on a parchment‑lined sheet and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 8‑10 minutes until the edges are lightly golden
  7. Step 7: let cool completely before piping royal icing and adding a dusting of powdered sugar.
💡 Chef's Tip: Use a silicone baking mat to prevent spreading and to achieve uniform browning.

Pair each cookie with a mug of peppermint hot chocolate for a festive dip; store leftovers in a zip‑top bag with a slice of bread to retain softness.

5. Peppermint Star, Tree & Snowflake Sugar Cookies

The star cookies bring a caramelized edge, the trees offer a nutty undertone from toasted almond slivers, and the snowflakes provide a delicate crunch from powdered sugar dusting. 👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  4. Step 4: fold in toasted almond slivers for added texture

  6. Step 6: preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment
  7. Step 7: roll dough to ¼‑inch thickness and cut star, tree, and snowflake shapes using appropriate cutters

💡 Chef's Tip: For extra shine, brush the finished cookies lightly with a thin glaze of melted white chocolate.

Each bite is crisp on the edges yet tender in the center, releasing a warm, buttery aroma that fills the kitchen. 🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 ½ cups all‑purpose flour
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 ½ tsp pure vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp almond extract
  • green food coloring (optional)
  • assorted rainbow sprinkles

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a large bowl, whisk together flour, a pinch of salt, and baking powder, then set aside
  2. Step 2: Cream the softened butter and granulated sugar together on medium speed until light and fluffy, about 2‑3 minutes, scraping down the sides as needed
  3. Step 3: Add the egg, vanilla, and almond extract, beating until fully incorporated, then gradually mix in the dry ingredients until a smooth dough forms
  4. Step 4: If desired, add a few drops of green food coloring and knead gently until the color is uniform, then shape the dough into a disc, wrap in plastic, and chill for at least 30 minutes to firm

  6. Step 6: Roll the dough to ¼‑inch thickness, cut into tree shapes using a festive cookie cutter, place on the sheet, and lightly press sprinkles onto the tops

  8. Step 8: Remove from oven, let cool on the sheet 2 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to finish cooling before serving.
💡 Chef's Tip: Chill the dough thoroughly—at least 30 minutes—to prevent spreading and ensure crisp, defined tree silhouettes.

Arrange the trees on a holiday platter with extra sprinkles for a pop of color; store any leftovers in an airtight tin at room temperature for up to one week.

Miniature gingerbread houses with warm spices and sweet frosting invite cozy nibbling.

These hand‑shaped cookies combine the classic gingerbread spice blend—cinnamon, ginger, clove, and nutmeg—with a buttery sugar‑cookie base, creating a fragrant, melt‑in‑the‑mouth treat. The edges are lightly crisp while the centers stay tender, and a thin vanilla glaze adds a glossy finish. The aroma of toasted spices fills the kitchen, making them an instant crowd‑pleaser at any holiday gathering. Their bite‑size size and decorative shape make them perfect for playful plating or a sweet “hand‑to‑hand” gift.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 ¼ cups all‑purpose flour
  • ½ tsp ground ginger
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground cloves
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1  tsp vanilla extract
  • royal icing for decorating

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, ginger, cinnamon, cloves, nutmeg, and a pinch of salt
  2. Step 2: In a stand mixer on medium speed, cream butter and brown sugar until light and fluffy, about 2‑3 minutes, scraping the bowl as needed
  3. Step 3: Beat in the egg and vanilla extract until fully combined, then gradually add the dry spice mixture, mixing until a cohesive dough forms
  4. Step 4: Divide the dough in half, shape each portion into a flat disc, wrap in plastic, and chill for at least 45 minutes to firm
  5. Step 5: Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment
  6. Step 6: Roll each chilled disc to ¼‑inch thickness, cut out small house shapes using a gingerbread‑house cutter, place on the sheets, and bake for 12‑15 minutes, or until the edges turn a light amber and the cookies are set
  7. Step 7: Cool completely on a wire rack before piping royal icing to assemble or decorate as desired.
💡 Chef's Tip: Freeze the rolled dough for 10‑15 minutes before cutting; this prevents the spices from smearing and yields cleaner edges.

Serve a handful on a rustic wooden board with hot cocoa, or store in an airtight container for up to 10 days; frozen cookies re‑heat perfectly in a 300°F oven for 5 minutes.

9. Classic Gingerbread Man Sugar Cookies Hand‑Held

⏱️ 38 Mins (15m prep, 23m bake) 💰 Budget‑Friendly (~$3.50/serving) 🍽️ Serves 10‑12
Save Pin
Playful gingerbread men with a crisp edge and soft center bring holiday cheer to any gathering.

The iconic gingerbread man combines a buttery sugar‑cookie base with a bold spice blend of ginger, cinnamon, and molasses, delivering a sweet‑spicy flavor that’s both nostalgic and comforting. The cookies are crisp along the edges yet remain delightfully chewy inside, releasing a warm, aromatic scent as they bake. A light dusting of powdered sugar adds a festive sparkle, while the classic shape makes them instantly recognizable. Perfect for kids and adults alike, they’re a timeless holiday staple.

🛒 Key Ingredients:

  • 2 ¼ cups all‑purpose flour
  • ½ tsp ground ginger
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground cloves
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • ¾ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup molasses
  • 1 large egg
  • 1  tsp vanilla extract
  • powdered sugar for dusting

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method & Directions:

  1. Step 1: Whisk together flour, ginger, cinnamon, cloves, baking soda, and a pinch of salt in a large bowl
  2. Step 2: In a stand mixer on medium speed, cream butter and brown sugar until pale and fluffy, about 2‑3 minutes, then add molasses and beat until fully incorporated
  3. Step 3: Add the egg and vanilla, mixing until smooth, then gradually add the dry flour mixture, mixing just until a soft dough forms
  4. Step 4: Divide the dough, flatten each half into a disk, wrap in plastic, and chill for at least 30 minutes
  5. Step 5: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment
  6. Step 6: Roll dough to ¼‑inch thickness, cut gingerbread‑man shapes, place on sheets, and bake 10‑12 minutes until edges are lightly browned and the centers have set
  7. Step 7: Transfer to a wire rack, let cool, then dust lightly with powdered sugar before serving.
💡 Chef's Tip: For extra chewiness, bake the cookies for the minimum time and let them finish cooking on the hot baking sheet after removal from the oven.

Pair with a mug of spiced hot cider or hot chocolate; freeze any leftovers—thaw and re‑warm briefly in a 300°F oven for a fresh‑out‑of‑the‑oven texture.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What foods should I prepare ahead of time?
A: Cold dips, truffle balls, snack mixes, and chocolate treats can easily be prepared 1-2 days in advance. Hot appetizers like wrapped sausages can be assembled ahead and baked fresh right as guests arrive.

Q: How much food should I plan per adult guest?
A: For a 2-3 hour cocktail party where a sit-down dinner is not served, plan on 6 to 8 appetizer bites per adult. If hosting during a traditional dinner hour, increase to 10 to 12 pieces per person.

Q: What foods can safely stay at room temperature?
A: Dry snacks and baked treats can stay out on the buffet table throughout the party. For perishable appetizers (cheeses, meats, and dips), adhere to the standard USDA 2-hour rule by serving in smaller batches replenished from the fridge.
