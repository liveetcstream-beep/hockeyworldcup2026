Best Homemade Lasagna Soup Recipe (One-Pot, Cheesy & Cozy)

Freshly prepared Best Homemade Lasagna Soup Recipe (One-Pot, Cheesy & Cozy) ready to serve.

Imagine capturing all the rich, decadent layers of traditional baked lasagna and transforming them into a bubbling, aromatic bowl of comforting soup. This best homemade lasagna soup recipe delivers everything you crave about the classic Italian casserole—seasoned ground beef, tender broken pasta, and robust crushed tomatoes in a deeply savory broth—all in one single pot. As it simmers on the stove, fragrant garlic, sweet onions, and Italian herbs fill your kitchen with an irresistible aroma that promises a wholesome, restaurant-quality meal.

What makes this recipe stand out is the brilliant textural contrast between the velvety tomato-infused broth, the al dente pasta ribbons, and the showstopping dollop of creamy ricotta-mozzarella mixture melting right into the hot soup. It gives you all the deeply satisfying flavor of a multi-step baking project with none of the hassle.

Whether you are pulling together a cozy weeknight dinner for a hungry family or feeding friends on a chilly weekend evening, this foolproof one-pot wonder comes together effortlessly in under forty minutes. Every spoonful delivers a hearty, cheesy embrace that will instantly make this your go-to comfort food favorite.

❤️ Why You'll Love This Recipe

⭐ Ready in under 40 minutes for effortless weeknight cooking
⏱️ Uses simple pantry staples and budget-friendly ingredients
💰 One-pot cleanup makes after-dinner chores a breeze
✨ Creamy ricotta cheese topping melts into a decadent restaurant-quality finish

🛒 Key Ingredients & Flavor Science

Core ingredients you'll need (exact measurements are in the recipe card below):

  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 1 large yellow onion, finely diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 4 cups low-sodium beef broth
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed Italian tomatoes
  • 8 oz mafaldine or lasagna noodles, broken into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 cup whole milk ricotta cheese
  • 1.5 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves, chopped

🔪 Essential Kitchen Equipment:

  • Heavy-duty roasting pan or cast iron skillet
  • Sharp chef's knife & sturdy cutting board
  • Pastry basting brush or silicone spatula
  • Digital instant-read meat thermometer

📸 Step-by-Step Visual Cooking Guide

Follow these chronological process steps with visual indicators and exact temperatures.

Step 1: Assemble and Prep the Fresh Ingredients

Step 1 of 6
Save Pin
Gather and prep all your fresh produce, herbs, canned goods, and proteins before firing up the stove.
Begin by organizing your workstation, ensuring all vegetables are washed, the garlic is minced, and the ground beef is ready to go. Break your lasagna noodles or mafaldine pasta into manageable bite-sized shards so they fit comfortably on your soup spoon later. Having every component measured out and within arm's reach ensures a smooth, stress-free cooking process once the heat is turned on. Take a moment to combine the ricotta, half of the mozzarella, and Parmesan in a small bowl, stirring until smooth to create your cheesy garnish.
💡 Chef's Technique Tip: Pre-measuring your ingredients (known in professional kitchens as mise en place) prevents frantic rushing and ensures even cooking times.

Step 2: Brown the Beef and Aromatics

Step 2 of 6
Save Pin
Brown the ground beef and aromatic onions over medium-high heat until deeply caramelized and fragrant.
Place your Dutch oven over medium-high heat and add two tablespoons of olive oil. Add the lean ground beef and diced yellow onions, breaking up the meat with a wooden spoon as it cooks. Sauté for 6 to 8 minutes until the beef is thoroughly browned and the onions are soft, translucent, and just beginning to caramelize at the edges. Drain off any excess grease if necessary, then stir in the minced garlic, tomato paste, dried Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes, cooking for about 1 minute until the garlic becomes deeply aromatic.
💡 Chef's Technique Tip: Let the ground beef develop a slight crust against the hot bottom of the pot before stirring to build incredible depth of flavor.

Step 3: Simmer the Rich Tomato Broth

Step 3 of 6
Save Pin
Pour in the crushed tomatoes and beef broth, letting the rich liquid simmer and develop its complex flavors.
Pour the low-sodium beef broth and the can of crushed Italian tomatoes directly into the pot with the seasoned beef mixture. Season with kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, stirring well to scrape up any delicious browned bits stuck to the bottom of the pan. Bring the liquid to a gentle boil over medium-high heat, then immediately reduce the heat to medium-low. Allow the soup to simmer uncovered for 10 minutes, letting the tomatoes mellow and the savory flavors meld together beautifully.
💡 Chef's Technique Tip: Scraping the bottom of the pot releases the fond, which acts as a natural flavor enhancer for the entire soup base.

Step 4: Cook the Pasta Right in the Broth

Step 4 of 6
Save Pin
Add the broken pasta pieces directly into the simmering broth until tender and al dente.
Drop the broken lasagna noodles or mafaldine pasta directly into the simmering tomato broth. Stir frequently during the first few minutes to prevent the pasta pieces from sticking together or clinging to the bottom of the pot. Maintain a gentle simmer over medium heat for about 8 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the pasta is cooked to a perfect al dente texture. If the broth reduces too quickly while the pasta cooks, splash in an extra half-cup of beef broth or water to keep the soup perfectly saucy.
💡 Chef's Technique Tip: Cook the pasta just until al dente because it will continue to soften slightly as the hot soup sits in the bowl.

Step 5: Prepare the Decadent Cheese Topping

Step 5 of 6
Save Pin
Mix creamy ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan together to create the ultimate lasagna cheese topping.
While the pasta finishes softening in the broth, finalize your creamy cheese dollop. In a small mixing bowl, combine the whole milk ricotta cheese, the remaining shredded mozzarella, and the freshly grated Parmesan cheese, stirring until thoroughly blended. Season the mixture with a tiny pinch of salt and black pepper if desired. This rich, cool-to-warm contrast will melt gorgeously when dropped onto the boiling-hot soup right before serving, mimicking the signature cheesy top layer of a baked lasagna.
💡 Chef's Technique Tip: Use whole milk ricotta rather than skim varieties for a richer, velvety texture that won't curdle when it hits the hot broth.

Step 6: Serve Hot and Enjoy

Step 6 of 6
Save Pin
Ladle the steaming lasagna soup into bowls and crown with a generous dollop of the cheesy ricotta mixture and fresh basil.
Ladle the piping hot, aromatic lasagna soup into deep serving bowls, ensuring every portion gets plenty of seasoned beef and tender pasta ribbons. Spoon a generous dollop of the prepared ricotta and mozzarella cheese mixture right into the center of each hot bowl. Garnish generously with freshly chopped basil leaves and an extra sprinkle of grated Parmesan cheese. Serve immediately while piping hot and encourage your guests to stir the melting cheese right into the rich broth.
💡 Chef's Technique Tip: Serve with warm, buttered garlic bread or crusty Italian bread to mop up every last drop of the savory broth.

👨‍🍳 Pro Chef Tips for Flawless Results

  • Brown the Beef Well: Allowing the meat to develop a deep brown crust builds the foundational savory flavor of the soup
  • Don't Overcook Pasta: Cook noodles directly in the broth only until al dente to prevent a mushy texture
  • Use Whole Milk Dairy: Opt for whole milk ricotta and fresh mozzarella for the creamiest, most authentic melt
  • Customize the Heat: Adjust the red pepper flakes up or down depending on your family's preferred spice level

❄️ Make-Ahead, Storage & Reheating

**Refrigerator:** Store cooled leftovers in an airtight glass container for up to 4 days. Note that the pasta will continue to absorb liquid as it sits, so you may need to add a splash of broth or water when reheating gently on the stovetop over low heat.
**Freezer:** For best results, freeze the soup base without the pasta and cheese for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, reheat on the stove, and cook fresh pasta right into the hot broth before serving.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: Can I prepare this recipe ahead of time? |

A: Yes, you can prep and season ingredients up to 24 hours in advance and keep them chilled in the refrigerator.

Q: What can I substitute for the dairy or heavy cream? |

A: Full-fat canned coconut milk or unsweetened cashew cream works wonderfully without sacrificing rich velvety texture.

Q: How do I ensure maximum tenderness? |

A: Avoid overcooking by verifying doneness with an instant-read thermometer (165°F / 74°C for poultry) and always allow 5 minutes resting time.

Q: What side dishes pair best with this meal? |

A: Serve alongside garlic butter pasta, fluffy mashed potatoes, roasted asparagus, or a crisp Caesar salad.
