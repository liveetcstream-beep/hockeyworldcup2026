🛒 Key Ingredients & Flavor Science

Core ingredients you'll need (exact measurements are in the recipe card below):

  • 1.0 lb lean ground beef
  • 0.5 lb ground pork
  • 0.5 cup plain breadcrumbs
  • 0.33 cup grated parmesan cheese, plus extra for serving
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 0.25 cup fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • 1.0 tsp kosher salt
  • 0.5 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 2.0 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, finely diced
  • 3 medium carrots, peeled and diced
  • 3 stalks celery, diced
  • 8.0 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 0.75 cup dry acini di pepe pasta
  • 5.0 oz fresh baby spinach leaves

🔪 Essential Kitchen Equipment:

  • 6-quart heavy-bottomed Dutch oven or stockpot
  • Chef's knife and wooden cutting board
  • Baking sheet lined with parchment paper
  • Fine-mesh stainless steel strainer

Step 2: Mix the Meatball Ingredients

Step 2 of 6
Save Pin
Combine ground beef, pork, breadcrumbs, cheese, herbs, and spices gently in a large mixing bowl.
In a large mixing bowl, combine the lean ground beef, ground pork, plain breadcrumbs, grated parmesan cheese, lightly beaten egg, minced garlic, chopped parsley, kosher salt, and black pepper. Using your clean hands, gently fold and mix the ingredients together until just combined. Be careful not to overwork the meat mixture, as aggressive kneading will break down the proteins and result in dense, rubbery meatballs rather than tender, melt-in-your-mouth bites.
💡 Chef's Technique Tip: Avoid overmixing the meatball combination; gentle handling keeps the texture light and tender rather than dense and tough.

Step 3: Roll the Mini Meatballs

Step 3 of 6
Save Pin
Roll the seasoned meat mixture into uniform, bite-sized mini meatballs on a parchment-lined tray.
Pinch off small portions of the meat mixture and roll them between your palms to form uniform, bite-sized mini meatballs measuring roughly half an inch to three-quarters of an inch in diameter. Arrange the rolled meatballs in neat rows on a parchment-lined baking sheet to prevent sticking. Keeping them small is essential to the classic style of Italian wedding soup, allowing them to fit comfortably on a soup spoon alongside broth and pasta.
💡 Chef's Technique Tip: Lightly wet your hands with cold water before rolling to prevent the meat mixture from sticking to your fingers.

Step 4: Simmer the Broth & Meatballs

Step 4 of 6
Save Pin
Gently drop the mini meatballs into the simmering chicken broth to cook through and flavor the base.
Heat your heavy-bottomed Dutch oven over medium heat with a splash of olive oil, and gently sauté the diced onions, carrots, and celery until softened and aromatic. Pour in the low-sodium chicken broth and bring the liquid up to a gentle simmer. Carefully drop the raw mini meatballs one by one into the simmering broth. Let them cook undisturbed for about 6 to 8 minutes until they float to the surface and are cooked completely through, infusing the broth with deep, savory flavor.
💡 Chef's Technique Tip: Drop meatballs into simmering liquid gently to avoid splashing and maintain a clear, beautiful broth.

Step 5: Wilt the Fresh Spinach

Step 5 of 6

Stir fresh baby spinach and pasta into the bubbling pot until the greens are tender and wilted.
Add the dry acini di pepe pasta directly into the simmering soup pot, stirring occasionally so the tiny pasta grains do not stick to the bottom of the pan. Cook for about 6 to 8 minutes until the pasta reaches al dente texture. Next, fold in the fresh baby spinach leaves, pressing them down gently into the hot broth. Allow the spinach to wilt down into vibrant emerald ribbons, which takes only 1 to 2 minutes, releasing its fresh earthy flavor into the soup.
💡 Chef's Technique Tip: Add the spinach at the very end of cooking to preserve its bright green color and fresh nutrient profile.

Step 6: Serve and Garnish

Ladle the piping hot, aromatic soup into warm serving bowls, making sure each portion gets an abundant share of tender mini meatballs, fluffy acini di pepe pasta, and vibrant greens suspended in the rich golden broth. Dust the surface generously with freshly grated parmesan cheese and cracked black pepper just before serving. Serve immediately alongside warm, crusty Italian bread to soak up every last drop of the delicious savory broth.
💡 Chef's Technique Tip: Serve immediately while piping hot, and offer extra grated parmesan cheese at the table for guests to customize their bowls.

👨‍🍳 Pro Chef Tips for Flawless Results

  • Room Temperature Start: Always let large cuts sit on the counter for 45-60 minutes before hitting heat for uniform cooking.
  • Layer Seasonings: Season lightly at each stage of the process to build deep, balanced complexity.
  • Control Caramelization: High-sugar glazes should be brushed during the final 15-20 minutes of baking to prevent burning.
  • Rest Before Slicing: Resting preserves internal juices, resulting in tender, succulent bites rather than dry slices.

❄️ Make-Ahead, Storage & Reheating

**Refrigerator:** Store cooled leftovers in an airtight glass container for up to 4 days. Note that the pasta will continue to absorb liquid as it sits; simply add a splash of chicken broth when reheating on the stove over medium-low heat.
**Freezer:** For best results, freeze the soup base, meatballs, and broth *without* the pasta in a sealed freezer-safe container for up to 3 months. Cook fresh pasta and add it when reheating after thawing overnight in the fridge.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: Can I prepare this recipe ahead of time? |

A: Yes, you can roll the meatballs and chop the vegetables up to 24 hours in advance and keep them stored separately in the refrigerator.

Q: What can I substitute for acini di pepe pasta? |

A: If you cannot find acini di pepe, small pasta shapes like pastina, ditalini, or orzo make wonderful substitutes.

Q: How do I ensure my meatballs stay tender? |

A: Use a mix of ground beef and pork, incorporate breadcrumbs soaked in milk or an egg binder, and avoid overworking the meat mixture during shaping.

Q: What side dishes pair best with this meal? |

A: Serve alongside warm garlic butter breadsticks, a crisp green salad with lemon vinaigrette, or roasted garlic parmesan asparagus.
