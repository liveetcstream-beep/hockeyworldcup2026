Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Welcome to Cozy Plate Recipes (https://hockeyworldcup2026schedule.com). This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we and third-party partners use them on our website, and your choices regarding cookie management.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, deliver relevant advertising, and provide reporting insights to website owners.

Cookies can be "session cookies" (which expire when you close your web browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain stored on your device until they reach a set expiration date or you manually delete them).

2. How Cozy Plate Recipes Uses Cookies

We use cookies for several vital purposes to ensure a seamless culinary browsing experience:

  • Essential / Necessary Cookies: These cookies are critical to enable core functionality, navigation, and security on our website. Without these, the site cannot function properly.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our recipe posts, popular search queries, bounce rates, and traffic sources. All data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense): Third-party advertising vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • Social Media & Sharing Cookies: When you save recipe pins or share meals across social platforms (like Pinterest or Facebook), social media widgets may set cookies to track interaction and attribution.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookie

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads to our users based on their browsing history across the web. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com.

Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at https://optout.networkadvertising.org or the Digital Advertising Alliance at https://aboutads.info/choices.

4. How You Can Control & Disable Cookies

You have the right to accept or decline cookies at any time. Most modern browsers allow you to modify cookie preferences within their settings or preferences panel:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Block all cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.

Please note: If you choose to block or delete essential cookies, certain features, recipe bookmarks, or interactive elements of Cozy Plate Recipes may not function as intended.

5. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect technological changes, legal compliance standards, or our operational practices. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
